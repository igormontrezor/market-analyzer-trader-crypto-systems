=== HIPER REPIQUE — LOGIC REFINEMENT (app.py) ===

Fixed Sharpe threshold: was incorrectly using sell=4.5, now correctly uses buy=-1.5
  - Before: _sh_b = (_sharpe < 4.5) & (_sharpe.shift(1) >= 4.5)  [wrong — this was the sell threshold]
  - After:  _sh_b = (_sharpe < -1.5) & (_sharpe.shift(1) <= -1.5) [correct — matches _cross_below in market_analysis_app.py]

Changed activation logic from AND to OR:
  - Before: _conf_b = _sh_b & _so_b  (both conditions required simultaneously)
  - After:  _conf_b = _sh_b | _so_b  (either Sharpe OR Sortino confirms the bottom)

Sortino condition unchanged: SMA fast(20) crosses above slow(70) on BTC daily — specific to daily timeframe

=== MONTHLY TOP 3 CONVICTION ENGINE (gems_ai_filter.py) ===

Complete rewrite of the monthly analysis cycle

New functions:
  _load_weekly_history()
    Loads last 8 weeks of weekly top picks from gems_ai_results.json

  _build_monthly_candidates()
    Phase 1: coins that appeared in weekly top 10 over last 4 weeks + top 15 from monthly scan
    Enriches each candidate with: weekly_freq, weekly_best_rank, weekly_avg_conf, recurring_flags

  _build_monthly_conviction_score()
    Phase 2: conviction score 0-100 with 5 weighted components:
      30% weekly frequency history
      25% mid-term fundamentals (drawdown recovery + smart_money_div + rank_up)
      20% silent accumulation (ratio + accumulation_score + vol_up)
      15% consistency and temporal trend (consistency_score + weekly_trend)
      10% historical AI confidence across previous weeks
    Bonuses: HOT_NARRATIVE+SMART_MONEY_DIV (+5), SELLER_EXHAUSTION (+4), max frequency (+5)

  _build_monthly_prompt()
    Phase 3: surgical prompt focused on 30-90 day timeframe
    Explicit priority criteria, monthly_thesis field, top3_comparison, sectors_in_focus

Weekly results now saved to weekly_history[] (last 8 entries) in gems_ai_results.json
Monthly result includes monthly_conviction score, weekly_frequency and timeframe per pick
